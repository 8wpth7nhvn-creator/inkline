Questions

Questions people ask.

Your story stays yours.

No account. Your CV is saved in your own browser, and Start over deletes it in one click.

Is Inkline really free?

Yes. Building, editing and downloading your CV costs nothing. There's no trial, no card and no watermark.

Will my CV sound like a robot wrote it?

No. The AI writes from your own answers and keeps your way of saying things. You can change any word before you download.

Can I upload my old CV?

Yes. Upload it as a PDF or Word file (OpenDocument, RTF and text files work too). Inkline reads it right in your browser, so it isn't sent anywhere, fills in a fresh CV, then asks what has changed since, like a new job. Made it in Pages? Export it as a PDF first.

Can I ask the AI questions while I make my CV?

Yes. Ask things like how long your CV should be, or how to explain a gap. It answers, then carries on with your CV. When you are done, it shows your CV in a few designs so you can pick one.

Do I have to use the AI?

No. You can fill in any template yourself, like a simple form, and watch your CV update as you type. You can switch between the AI and the form at any time.

Can I add my photo?

Yes. Upload a photo and it appears in every design with a photo spot. It stays in your browser and is never sent to the AI. In the US and UK, CVs usually leave the photo out. In much of Europe, the Middle East and Asia, employers often expect one.

Will it get past hiring software?

For online application forms, choose a Classic design. They are one clean column of real text, the layout hiring software reads best. The colourful designs are made for emailing or handing in your CV, where a person reads it first.

I don't have much work experience. Can I still make one?

Yes. The AI asks about school, volunteering, side projects, part-time work and skills, so a first CV still looks full and confident.

What happens to my information?

Your CV is saved in your own browser, not in an account. While the AI is helping, your answers are sent to it only to write your CV. Start over deletes everything from your browser.

Can I make my CV in another language?

Yes. Write to the AI in your language and it replies, and writes your CV, in that language too.

Can I come back and edit it later?

Yes. Your CV stays saved in the browser you made it in. Open Inkline again and carry on where you left off.
