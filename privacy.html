1. Who is responsible

Inkline is run by Modar Abou Shahin, based in the Netherlands. I am responsible for your personal information under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Contact: a dedicated email address for privacy questions is being set up and will appear here before the AI interviewer or any paid feature is switched on. Until then, the website doesn't collect personal information about you, so there is nothing stored with us to look up or delete.

2. What stays on your device

To keep your work safe between visits, the CV maker saves these in your browser's own storage on your device:

  • your CV text, the messages shown in the chat, and your choices (design, colour, font, sections, icons)
  • your photo, and the uncropped photo so you can crop it again
  • the file the text reader needs to read photos of CVs (no personal information)

We can't see any of this. It never leaves your device unless you use the AI interviewer (section 4). To delete it, press Start over in the CV maker, or clear this website's data in your browser settings.

This storage is needed to give you the service you asked for, so the law doesn't require a cookie banner for it. It isn't used to track you.

3. Visiting the website

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web host, GitHub processes technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.

We don't use analytics, advertising, social media buttons or any other tracking. If that ever changes, we'll ask for your permission first.

4. When you use the AI interviewer

This applies only once the AI interviewer is switched on (see the box at the top), and only if you choose to use it. You can always make your CV without the AI.

What we receive

  • the messages you type in the chat and the CV as it stands
  • an old CV you upload while the AI is on. The file itself is passed on to the AI and not stored by us; the details read from it are kept with your chat (see below).

We don't receive your photo. Please don't put sensitive information in your CV that you don't need, such as your health, religion, ID or passport numbers.

What happens with it

  • Our server keeps your chat for up to 24 hours so the conversation can continue, then deletes it automatically. Pressing Start over deletes it straight away.
  • An AI company writes the replies and the CV text. We use one of these: OpenAI (the maker of ChatGPT) or Anthropic (the maker of Claude). The one in use will be named in the box at the top. We use their business service, whose terms don't let them train their AI on your information. They may keep it for a short time to check for misuse, as their terms describe.
  • To stop abuse and keep costs under control, we keep a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address with counters of how often the AI was used. Counters are deleted after 3 days. Our technical logs record events like "a reply was written" and how long it took, never what you wrote. Logs are deleted after 30 days.

Why we may do this

We use what you send to provide the service you asked for: writing your CV (GDPR article 6(1)(b)). The scrambled IP address, counters and logs protect the service from misuse (legitimate interest, article 6(1)(f)).

The AI never makes decisions about you. It only suggests text that you can change.

5. When you buy something

This applies only once paid features exist. Inkline is free right now.

  • A payment company (such as Stripe or Mollie) handles your payment. We never see or store your full card or bank details. The company will be named here before payments start.
  • We receive what we need for the order and the law: your name, email address, what you bought, the amount, the date and a payment reference.
  • Dutch tax law requires us to keep these records for 7 years.
  • We use this to deliver what you bought and handle refunds (article 6(1)(b)), and to meet tax law (article 6(1)(c)).

6. Who else receives information

Only the companies named above that help run the service: the web host, the AI company (if you use the AI) and the payment company (if you buy something). We never sell your information or share it for advertising.

Some of these companies are in the USA. Where information goes outside the EU, it is protected by the safeguards EU law requires, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Children

Anyone can make a CV with Inkline without the AI. The AI interviewer and paid features are for people aged 16 and over, or younger with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you think a child under 16 sent us information without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, ask us to limit or stop using it, object to how we use it, or receive a copy of it. Because we keep so little, you can do most of this yourself: your CV lives in your browser, and Start over deletes your AI chat from our server.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

10. How we protect it

  • The connection to the website is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Keys for the AI service are kept on the server, never in the website's code.
  • Uploaded files are checked and never saved. Logs never contain what you wrote.
  • Limits on use protect against spam and misuse.

11. Changes to this policy

When something changes, for example when the AI interviewer or payments are switched on, we update this page and the date at the top. Important changes will also be shown on the website.
